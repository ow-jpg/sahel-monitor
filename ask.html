Your OpenRouter key

This page calls the model from your browser, so it needs a key. The key is stored in this browser only. It never goes into the repository and nobody else visiting the site can see or use it. Get one free at openrouter.ai/keys, no card required. If you clear your browser data you will need to paste it again.

The model only sees items the search below your question managed to find, so a thin answer may mean thin retrieval rather than nothing having happened. Open the retrieval panel under any answer to see exactly what was sent. The model has no access to the web here, and anything it adds from its own training is guesswork about a fast-moving region, so check the linked reporting before using any of it.
