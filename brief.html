Summary

Written from headlines, not from articles. The model that wrote this has read each headline, the short blurb the feed carried, and the numbers around them: how many outlets ran each story, what is new, and how each country compares with its own recent volume. It has not opened a single article. So this describes the coverage rather than the events, and it cannot tell you why something happened or what it means. Use it to decide what to open.
